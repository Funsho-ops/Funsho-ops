## 🚀 About Me 
I'm a Blockchain Data Analyst and Web3 Growth Strategist based in Lagos, Nigeria. I bridge raw on-chain and off-chain data to actionable business insights, tokenomics models, and DeFi strategy across the Solana ecosystem.

My focus is on building dashboards that tell a clear story, analysing on-chain metrics that drive protocol decisions, and helping Web3 teams understand their users through data, not guesswork.

When I'm not working with data, I'm building communities, speaking at ecosystem events, and writing about Web3 infrastructure on The Builder's Edge. I live for the moment on-chain data reveals what no one else has noticed yet.

When I'm not working with data, I enjoy gaming and spending time with my family. I love the "aha!" moment when data reveals something new and useful.
